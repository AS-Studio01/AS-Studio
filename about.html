The Practice

Small by design, built for permanence.

AS Studio is an independent web design and front-end engineering studio based in India, partnering directly with founders, clinics, legal chambers, and hospitality venues worldwide.

01 · WHY WE WORK THIS WAY

No account managers between you and the code.

Most small business sites fail for predictable reasons: oversized templates that take four seconds to render on mobile, generic filler copy, and monthly maintenance fees for changes that take ten minutes. At AS Studio, the person reading your brief designs the typography, writes the markup, tests the phone viewport, and ships your repository.

02 · ENGINEERING DISCIPLINE

Clean static foundations over heavy stacks.

Unless your site genuinely needs dynamic databases, shipping semantic HTML, modern CSS custom properties, zero-secret client scripts, and concise serverless API endpoints gives you sub-second loads, predictable security headers, and zero framework churn five years from now.

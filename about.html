AS Studio is an independent digital practice designing and engineering websites for ambitious businesses that have outgrown generic templates and want their online presence to feel as credible as the work behind it.

Who We Are

A focused studio, not a bloated agency.

We are independent designers and engineers who believe that quiet confidence beats digital noise. We do not maintain expensive downtown offices, account managers, or junior delegation layers.

When you collaborate with AS Studio, you speak directly with the senior practitioners who write the code, refine the typography, and architect the mobile experience. This eliminates weeks of miscommunication and ensures that the finished site reflects your exact standards.

Based in India, we collaborate with clients across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia—adapting seamlessly to international timezones and client workflows.

How We Work

A disciplined sprint, from first brief to verified launch.

PHASE 01

Discovery & Offer

We isolate what makes your business credible, analyze client questions, and structure the content architecture.

PHASE 02

Direction & Layout

We design considered, editorial layouts centered around mobile legibility, clear hierarchy, and natural reading pace.

PHASE 03

Precision Build

Fast, maintainable code with sub-second asset loading, zero framework bloat, and verified search signals.

PHASE 04

Phone Staging & QA

You test the live site on your personal phone, review every interaction, and receive full Git source handover.

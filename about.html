About

A small studio with a long view.

AS Studio is an independent web design studio. I build calm, fast websites for businesses that want to look established, and I keep the process simple enough that you always know what happens next.

How I work

Four rules I do not bend.

One person, one inbox

You talk to the person doing the work. No account managers, no hand-offs, no lost messages.

Clear scope, in writing

You agree what you get and by when before work starts. Anything extra is agreed before it is built.

Phone first, then desktop

Most visitors arrive on a phone, so that is where every page is designed and tested first.

You own everything

Every source file is handed over at the end. There is no licence fee and no lock-in to me.

Who it suits

Businesses that need to be found and trusted.

Local clinics, cafés, studios, firms and small shops. If a customer decides whether to contact you after a few seconds on your site, this is built for you.

Is $95 a real price for a real website?

Yes. Starter is one focused page, built to the same standard as the bigger packages. It is smaller, not cheaper in quality. Most local businesses that only need to be found and contacted start here.

Do I own the website?

You do. You get the full source files at handover, with no builder lock-in and no monthly licence to us.

Will it work on my phone and my customers' phones?

We design for phones first and test the finished site on a real phone before you pay the final amount. Speed optimization is included in every package.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, a few lines about what you do, your logo if you have one, and any photos you like. If you have none, we can work with simple imagery and you can swap in real photos later.

Yes. Many clients start on Starter and move to Growth when they want leads, or to Signature when they want to take payments.

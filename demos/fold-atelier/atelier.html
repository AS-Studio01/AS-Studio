Heritage & Process

Cloth that remembers hands.

Every Fold Atelier garment begins with unbleached fibre. We do not use industrial finishing mills; instead, our fabrics soften under running water, sunlight, and time.

Pillars

Three commitments to slow making.

Botanical Dye Vats

True indigo leaves, pomegranate rind, harda seeds, and madder root. Zero chemical run-off, zero synthetic fixing salts.

Handloom Weaving

Slow pit-loom and frame-loom weaves preserving individual yarn tension and distinct organic selvages.

Mending for Life

Every garment includes lifetime repair service. Send us worn elbows or torn hems and we hand-darn them at our workshop.

Private Studio Visit

Experience cloth in person.

Our fitting rooms in Pune and Jaipur are open by appointment for private consultations, fabric swatching, and bespoke measurements.

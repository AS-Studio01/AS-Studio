The atelier

A small studio with big patience.

FOLD began in a sunlit room in Koregaon Park with one vintage sewing table, a single pattern cutter, and a belief that clothing should outlast seasonal whims. Today our team is twelve, our workspace has grown, but the deliberate pace remains unchanged.

Made in runs of twelve

We cut twelve garments at a time. This guarantees zero warehouse deadstock, zero fabric waste, and complete pride in every seam.

Natural fibers only

Unbleached linen, organic ring-spun cotton, and heritage hand-loomed wool. No petrochemical polyesters or synthetic finishes.

Complimentary repairs

Lost a corozo button, tore a hem, or need a waistband adjusted? Send any FOLD piece back to our studio and we repair it free.

Book a fitting

Try it on, in the atelier.

Private fitting sessions are complimentary and take 30 minutes. We prepare your selected pieces in advance, steam them fresh, and brew a pot of fresh tea.

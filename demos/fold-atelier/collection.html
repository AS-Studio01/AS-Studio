Fit and care

Before you order.

How does the sizing run?

Relaxed fits, true to size. If you are between sizes, take the smaller for a neater line or the larger for an easy drape.

How do I care for linen?

Machine wash cool, hang to dry, and iron slightly damp if you like a crisp finish. It softens with every wash.

Can I return a piece?

Yes. Unworn pieces can be returned within 14 days for a refund or exchange.

Do you make to measure?

Yes, on request. Book a fitting and the atelier will take your measurements.

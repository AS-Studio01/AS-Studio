Fit and care

Before you order.

How does the sizing run?

Our garments are patterned with relaxed ease, true to size. If you are between sizes, take the smaller for a neater tailored line or the larger for an easy flowing drape. Check our Size Guide on any card for exact measurements.

How do I care for linen?

Machine wash cool on a gentle spin, hang to line dry, and steam or iron slightly damp if you prefer a crisp finish. Natural linen softens and gains character with every wash.

Can I return or exchange a piece?

Yes. Unworn pieces with original tags can be returned within 14 days for a replacement size or full refund.

Do you make to measure?

Yes, on request. Book a complimentary fitting at our Pune atelier and our pattern cutter will record your bespoke measurements.

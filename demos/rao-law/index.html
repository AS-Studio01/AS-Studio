Clear Answers

Frequently asked questions.

How are preliminary briefings conducted?

Preliminary conferences occur at our Nariman Point or Pune chambers, or via encrypted video link. During this 45-minute briefing, we review the dispute chronology and advise whether legal remedies or settlement dialogue best protects your business.

Do you handle institutional arbitrations outside India?

Yes. Our partners have appeared before the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) and the London Court of International Arbitration (LCIA) in cross-border joint venture contracts governed by Indian substantive law.

How are commercial retainers and fees structured?

Fees are structured on transparent fixed stages (pre-litigation notice, court motion hearings, tribunal sessions) agreed in advance. We do not charge unexpected administrative costs or billable surprises.

What preparation is required before an initial consultation?

Having a copy of the executed contract, dispute escalation clause, notice history, and an estimated claim valuation helps us evaluate your legal standing immediately.

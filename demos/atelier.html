Our makers

We work with seven workshops, not seven factories.

Each piece comes from a workshop we have visited, in Khurja, Bhuj, Jaipur and Auroville. We pay before the run begins, not after it sells, and we buy the whole run so nobody is left holding stock.

That is why quantities are small and reorders are slow. It is also why the person who made your vase was paid properly for it.

Care & returns

Ceramics

Dishwasher safe on a low setting, though hand washing keeps the glaze brighter for longer. Do not put them in the microwave if there is a metallic lustre.

Textiles

Cold machine wash, gentle cycle, no bleach. Expect a little shrinkage on the first wash — that is the nature of handloom cotton, not a fault.

Returns

Fourteen days from delivery, unused and in original packaging. We cover return shipping if the piece arrived damaged or differs from the listing.

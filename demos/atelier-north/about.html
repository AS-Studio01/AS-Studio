Studio Ethos

Made slowly, on purpose.

Atelier North collaborates with family-owned timber mills, brass turners and indigenous loom collectives across India. We believe that furniture should settle into a home with quiet confidence, acquiring depth and character over decades rather than wearing out.

Our Commitments

Built on enduring foundations.

01 · Provenance

Independent Workshops

Every piece is made in limited numbered editions by master craftspeople who earn fair, dignified living wages. No high-volume factory lines.

02 · Purity

Honest Natural Materials

Solid reclaimed teak, Belgian flax linen, unlacquered brass and raw stone. We refuse petroleum-based laminates and toxic chemical sealants.

03 · Longevity

Lifetime Service & Spares

We supply replacement linen covers, brass screws, and re-weaving cord services for life. Good design should never end up in a landfill.

Tactile Design

The joy of unhurried spaces.

Modern living is filled with disposable distractions. Our pieces are quiet invitations to sit down with a book, share a simple meal, or let morning sunlight move across warm wood grain.

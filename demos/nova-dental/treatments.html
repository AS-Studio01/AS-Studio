Insurance & Payment Support

Financing & insurance questions.

How does insurance reimbursement work for treatments?

We generate standardized diagnostic forms, digital radiograph prints, and signed clinical certificates aligned with Indian corporate health policies. Most corporate policies reimburse outpatient dental procedures like scaling and root canals within 7 to 14 days.

Do you provide 0% interest monthly instalment options?

Yes. For aligners, smile makeovers and implant procedures, we facilitate interest-free split payment plans spanning 3, 6 or 9 months with zero paperwork hassle.

Are emergency appointments available on weekends?

Yes. We hold slots for sudden acute toothache, crown dislodgement, or sports injuries on Saturdays and Sundays. Please phone ahead so our clinical team can prepare the operatory.

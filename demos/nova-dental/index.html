Insurance and payment questions.

Do you accept corporate dental insurance or cashless claims?

Yes. We provide itemized receipts, diagnostic coding and clinical certificates compatible with major Indian corporate medical insurers and reimbursement providers.

Are 0% interest monthly instalment options available?

We partner with leading healthcare financing providers to offer 3, 6, and 12-month interest-free instalment options on comprehensive treatments such as implants and clear aligners.

What happens if I experience sudden dental pain or an emergency?

We preserve dedicated emergency reserve slots every morning and afternoon. Call our clinic line and our reception team will accommodate you on the same day.

Always. Following your initial visual examination and digital scan, you receive a transparent, printed fee breakdown. You decide which steps to proceed with at your own pace.

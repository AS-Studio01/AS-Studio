Cancellation, Refund & Return Policy

Advance payment

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, projects begin with a 50% advance and the remaining 50% is due according to the agreed delivery and acceptance terms.

Client cancellation

A cancellation requested within 2 calendar days may be considered for a refund after deductions for work already completed and non-recoverable third-party or transaction costs. After that period, the advance is generally non-refundable to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Digital services

Website design and development are customized digital services and are not physically returnable. Where a service has been properly completed and delivered, a physical return is not applicable.

Studio cancellation

If AS Studio must cancel before completion, any refund will be handled according to the written project agreement and applicable law.

Cancellation, Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Standard payment structure

Unless a written proposal says otherwise, a project begins with a 50% advance and the remaining 50% is payable according to the agreed delivery or acceptance terms.

2. Client cancellation

A cancellation request made within two calendar days of payment may be considered for a refund after deducting work already completed and non-recoverable transaction or third-party costs. After that period, the advance is generally non-refundable to the extent permitted by law because project capacity, design time and development work may already have been committed.

3. Work already completed

If cancellation occurs after substantial work has started, AS Studio may retain amounts reasonably attributable to completed work, reserved capacity and non-recoverable costs.

4. Digital services

Web design and development are customized digital services. A completed digital deliverable is not a physical product that can be returned. Defects against the agreed scope are handled through the project review and correction process.

5. Studio cancellation

If AS Studio must cancel a project before completion for reasons within its control, the studio will work with the client on an appropriate resolution, which may include refunding amounts for undelivered work, subject to the written agreement and applicable law.

6. Contact

Cancellation or refund requests should be sent to asbusinessofficial01@gmail.com with the project name and relevant payment details.

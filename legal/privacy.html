Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us: your name, business name, email address or contact handle, budget indication and project details when you use the contact form, Telegram or email.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry, plan and deliver your project, and keep a normal business record of the work. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Contact-form briefs are transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored in Google Firebase (Firestore). The database is not publicly readable: only the studio owner, authenticated with a password, can access submitted briefs.

Service providers

Our website is hosted on Vercel. Contact submissions may be stored in Google Firebase. Both process data under their own privacy and security terms. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Orders on Fiverr and Upwork

Orders placed through Fiverr or Upwork are also handled under those platforms' own terms and privacy policies, which govern the payment and messaging data they hold.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. A single preference value may be stored in your browser's local storage to remember your chosen colour theme. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Retention and your choices

We keep information only as long as needed for the project and for legal or accounting requirements. You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing asbusinessofficial01@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable period.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights over your personal data, including the right to object to processing or to request a portable copy. Contact us and we will help.

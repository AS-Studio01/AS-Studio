Privacy Policy

AS Studio collects only the minimum details necessary to respond to project briefs and deliver commissioned websites.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit the inquiry form on our contact page, we collect your name, business name, contact handle (email or Telegram), package choice, timeline, and written brief. We do not use advertising pixels, behavioral tracking cookies, or third-party data brokers.

2. How Data Is Stored & Protected

Submitted briefs are processed exclusively by server-side API endpoints and stored with restricted service-account credentials. Browser clients have zero direct database read/write permissions. Admin sessions rely strictly on signed HttpOnly cookies.

3. Client Storage & Preferences

We store only your visual theme choice (light or dark) locally in browser storage (`localStorage`) so pages keep your preferred reading contrast across visits.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain active inquiry records only for scoping and project communication. You may request export or permanent deletion of your inquiry record at any time by writing to asbusinessofficial01@gmail.com.

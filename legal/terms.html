Terms of Service

Clear expectations for scope, timeline, revisions, and source ownership on every AS Studio commission.

1. Scope & Kickoff

Every engagement begins with a written scope summarizing page count, interactive features, fixed fee, and target staging date. Work begins once kickoff payment or escrow milestone funding is confirmed.

2. Revisions & Approval

Starter includes two structured revision rounds; Growth and Signature include three revision rounds. Feedback is tested against the live staging URL before final asset delivery.

3. Code & Design Ownership

Upon final settlement, full copyright to bespoke layouts, copy edits, stylesheets, and code created for your commission transfers 100% to you. Open-source font licenses remain under their respective SIL/Apache licenses.

4. 30-Day Post-Launch Warranty

For 30 calendar days after launch, layout regressions, responsive issues, or script bugs discovered on modern browsers are repaired at zero additional cost.

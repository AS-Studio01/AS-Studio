Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellation & refund

Last updated 15 November 2026

We want you to feel completely safe ordering. This policy explains exactly when a refund is available.

Before work begins

If you cancel before we have started design or development, you receive a full refund of any amount paid, less any non-recoverable third-party costs already incurred on your instruction (for example a domain or hosting purchase). We will always tell you first if such a cost exists.

After work has started

If you cancel once design or development is underway, we refund a fair proportion based on how much of the work is complete. We will show you exactly what has been produced and explain the calculation. As a guide:

  • Design stage: usually 50–75% refundable, depending on pages completed.
  • Build stage: usually 25–50% refundable.
  • Delivered and approved: not refundable, but all 30 days of included fixes still apply.

If we cannot deliver

If AS Studio is unable to deliver the agreed work for any reason on our side, you receive a full refund of amounts paid for undelivered work. This is unconditional.

Revisions instead of refunds

Most dissatisfaction is solved by revision, not refund. Every package includes a defined number of revision cycles, and we genuinely want the result to be right. Tell us what is wrong and we will fix it.

Orders through Fiverr and Upwork

Where you ordered through Fiverr or Upwork, refunds are processed through that platform's own dispute and escrow process, which takes precedence over this policy for those orders. Both platforms hold your money in escrow until you approve delivery.

Direct payments

For direct bank or UPI payments, refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 business days of agreement.

Domain, hosting and third-party costs

Amounts already paid to third parties — domain registrars, hosting providers, font or asset licences — are governed by those providers' policies and cannot be refunded by us once purchased.

How to request a refund

Email asbusinessofficial01@gmail.com with your project name and the reason. We reply within one business day and aim to resolve every request within 7 days.

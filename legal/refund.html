Cancellation & Refund Policy

Fair milestone-based cancellation terms protecting both client budget and reserved studio production days.

1. Before Work Starts

If a commission is cancelled before design and coding commence (within 24 hours of deposit payment), 100% of the deposit is refunded minus payment gateway transaction fees if applicable.

2. During Staging Production

Once architecture and staging builds are underway, completed milestone deliverables are non-refundable, while any unstarted milestone phases remain cancelled at zero future obligation.

3. Platform Escrow Orders

Orders placed through Fiverr or Upwork remain governed by their respective platform escrow and dispute resolution policies alongside our written delivery milestones.

4. Final Handoff

Once final source files and deployment verification are approved and transferred, engagements are complete and non-refundable; bug fixes remain covered under the 30-day warranty.

Case study

VitalCare Multispecialty Clinic

A patient-centered healthcare clinic concept built across multiple pages to reduce clinical anxiety, showcase medical specialists, and streamline appointment booking.

Concept projectThis clinic concept is fictional, created by AS Studio to show what a production-ready medical practice website delivers.
The brief

Trust, clarity, and unhurried care.

The brief was to design a comprehensive, accessible multi-page website for a private multispecialty medical center. It needed to outline six clinical departments (General Medicine, Paediatrics, Dermatology, Physiotherapy, Diagnostics, and Dental Care), present consultant credentials, provide transparent healthcare information, and offer a calm, accessible online booking enquiry journey.

My approach

Gentle pacing and reassuring architecture.

  • Layout: A clean multi-page hierarchy separating Clinic Overview, Six Clinical Care Areas, Clinician Team Roster, Appointment Enquiry, and FAQ Accordions.
  • Tone: Calm, reassuring, and dignified with warm sage and natural cream backgrounds, high-contrast readable typography, and accessible keyboard focus navigation.
  • Phone-first decisions: Sticky mobile emergency consultation banner, one-tap phone contact, and zero-distraction appointment form with real-time character counters.
What was built

Real features in the live concept.

Pages built6 pages: Home, Services, Team, Appointments, FAQs, Contact
Specialty departments6 clinical areas with care breakdowns, consultation expectations & fees
Online appointment enquirySpecialty selector, deep-linking URL parameters, real-time feedback
Clinician rosterPhysician profiles with qualifications, specialisms, and clinical philosophy
Patient FAQNative disclosure accordions addressing appointments, referrals & insurance

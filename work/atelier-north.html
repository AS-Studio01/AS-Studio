Case study

Atelier North

A serene e-commerce storefront for home goods built across six pages with filterable catalog, sliding cart, and checkout flow.

Concept projectThis business is fictional, created by AS Studio to show what a production-ready website delivers.
The brief

Calm, tactile e-commerce for home objects.

The brief was to design a tranquil, tactile 6-page e-commerce website for a curated home goods and Scandinavian furniture studio. It needed to provide an organized product catalog with category filters, detailed item specifications, an interactive sliding drawer cart, and a transparent multi-step checkout experience.

My approach

Tactile materials, clean typography, and zero friction.

  • Layout: A 6-page boutique storefront featuring curated collections, deep product detail pages, and a distraction-free checkout flow.
  • Tone: Scandinavian serenity pairing washed linen backgrounds with Fraunces serif headings and crisp product photography.
  • Phone-first decisions: A smooth slide-out cart drawer, persistent mobile order actions, and compact product cards built for touch interaction.

Case study

Apex Residences

A luxury architectural development concept crafted across six pages with floorplans, journal narratives, and private tour booking.

Concept projectThis business is fictional, created by AS Studio to show what a production-ready website delivers.
The brief

Cinematic presence for luxury real estate.

The brief was to design an immersive 6-page website for an architectural residential property development to capture discerning buyers and design-conscious investors. It needed to present floorplans, penthouse residences, material specifications, architectural journal stories, and a confidential viewing reservation system.

My approach

Scale, atmosphere, and seamless navigation.

  • Layout: A 6-page ecosystem balancing expansive full-bleed heroes with structured specification tables and residence indices.
  • Tone: Architectural majesty pairing deep basalt darks with bronze accents and generous editorial margins.
  • Phone-first decisions: Fast-loading progressive WebP images, mobile-optimized floorplan tables, and a dedicated thumb-friendly viewing reservation form.

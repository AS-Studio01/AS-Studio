Case study

The AS Studio website.

My own site had one job: prove the work. This is how it was built and what it weighs.

The brief

A studio site that does what it sells.

It needed to feel calm and established, load fast on a phone, explain three packages clearly and make one action obvious: start a project.

The idea

A website is a front door.

The home page walks you through an archway into a courtyard as you scroll. The arch then becomes the shape of the packages and the logo.

The hero

Scroll is the timeline.

A ten second shot, generated in Google Flow, is scrubbed frame by frame as you scroll. It is downloaded in the background so the page is usable straight away. Phones, small windows and anyone who asks their device for reduced motion get a still image and the same words instead.

The shot

Where the scroll begins and ends.

Measured

What it weighs.

Sizes measured from the built files. Real-world speed also depends on hosting and connection.

Built to be used

Accessible by default.

Every page has a skip link, visible keyboard focus and real headings. Motion respects the reduced-motion setting, caption contrast over the video is checked frame by frame, and the contact form works with a keyboard and falls back to email if sending fails.

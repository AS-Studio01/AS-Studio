Case study

Rao & Partners

A prestigious dispute resolution and commercial litigation counsel concept designed for clarity, trust, and confidentiality.

Concept projectThis business is fictional, created by AS Studio to show what a production-ready website delivers.
The brief

Authority and discretion for legal counsel.

The brief was to design an authoritative single-page website for a commercial litigation firm that balances prestige with approachable counsel. It needed to communicate practice specializations, outline dispute resolution areas, and provide a direct brief intake mechanism for corporate clients.

My approach

Dignified typography and clean hierarchy.

  • Layout: A structured vertical hierarchy dividing practice disciplines, partner profiles, and brief intake with clean dividers.
  • Tone: Restrained dignity featuring Cinzel serif typography, dark obsidian backgrounds, and muted gold highlights.
  • Phone-first decisions: Direct one-tap contact pathways, legible body text across mobile screens, and zero heavy asset bloat.

Case study

Nova Dental Care

A patient-centered healthcare clinic concept built across three pages to reduce anxiety and streamline online appointment booking.

Concept projectThis business is fictional, created by AS Studio to show what a production-ready website delivers.
The brief

Calm, clarity, and transparent care.

The brief was to design a reassuring 3-page website for a modern dental clinic that alleviates patient anxiety and clarifies care fees upfront. It needed to outline preventative and restorative treatments with transparent fees, highlight doctor qualifications, and provide a frictionless appointment booking process.

My approach

Gentle pacing and transparent information.

  • Layout: A logical 3-page flow separating Clinic Overview, Treatments Directory with fee guidelines, and Online Booking.
  • Tone: Clean, reassuring, and approachable with calming mint and sage tones, gentle curves, and conversational explanations.
  • Phone-first decisions: Persistent mobile book button, finger-friendly appointment date selectors, and quick emergency contact options.
